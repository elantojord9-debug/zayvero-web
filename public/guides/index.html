TRAVEL IDEAS

Make a better day of it.

Big adventures are not required. Explore cities thoughtfully, even with a small budget.

Plan a low-cost city day

Start with a publicly accessible park or waterfront. Check official opening and entry information; parking and nearby attractions may cost extra.

Choose two places, not ten

In large cities, travel distances can be substantial. Two well-chosen stops often make a better experience than an exhausting checklist.

Have a weather backup

Check the forecast and plan an indoor alternative when needed. Verify its ticket and opening policies.
