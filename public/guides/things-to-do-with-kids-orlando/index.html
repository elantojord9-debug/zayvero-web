Orlando With Kids Beyond Theme Parks: 5 Family Ideas

You do not need a theme-park budget to plan a family afternoon in Orlando. A downtown lake with playgrounds, an interactive museum, a science-oriented indoor outing and nature-focused spaces offer different options for young travelers. The best plan depends on children's ages, how much walking they can enjoy and how easily your group can get across town.

5 researched placesOutdoor & indoor alternativesUpdated November 8, 2026
Family planning note

Check each operator's official website for age suitability, opening hours, safety rules, admissions and reservations. Our descriptions do not imply that every location is free or that we have personally visited it.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

What makes sense for your family?

01 · Playgrounds and paved downtown walk

Lake Eola Park

Free public park; paddle boats cost extra

Orlando's official parks directory lists play areas for younger and older children, restrooms, a walking path and lake views. Swan boat rentals are optional and paid; you can enjoy a stroll and playground without renting one. Keep a close eye on children around the water.

02 · Interactive exhibits and learning

Orlando Science Center

Ticketed museum

A strong indoor option for science-minded families, with activities that vary by exhibition and age group. Choose it as the main destination rather than trying to squeeze a museum visit into a short break between distant outdoor stops. Confirm admission and program requirements.

Official visitor information ↗Find on Maps ↗
03 · Garden exploration

Harry P. Leu Gardens

Paid admission except specific programs; verify

Landscaped grounds can be an enjoyable outing for children who like nature and have the stamina to walk. It is not a theme park, and expectations should be set accordingly. Review current visitor guidance, weather and any special events.

Official visitor information ↗Find on Maps ↗
04 · Wildlife observation and trails

Orlando Wetlands

Free public entry; travel required

The City of Orlando operates this nature area outside central Orlando. It's best for families interested in wildlife and outdoor learning, not as a short stop between downtown attractions. Prepare for heat, insects and changing trail conditions.

Official visitor information ↗Find on Maps ↗
05 · Quiet city nature walk

Greenwood Urban Wetlands

Public park

This smaller natural setting is a calm alternative to larger attractions and can fit families who enjoy bird-watching or short walks. Check the exact park access, weather and available amenities before leaving.

Official visitor information ↗Find on Maps ↗
A BETTER FAMILY DAY

Three steps before you leave.

  1. For younger children, start with Lake Eola playgrounds and avoid planning a long walking route.
  2. On rainy or very hot days, make Orlando Science Center your primary experience if tickets fit your budget.
  3. Choose Orlando Wetlands only if transportation and your family's outdoor interests make the separate trip worthwhile.
