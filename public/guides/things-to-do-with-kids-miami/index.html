TRAVEL WITH KIDS · FLORIDA

Things to Do With Kids in Miami: Parks, Art & Rainy-Day Ideas

Miami with children can mean waterfront play, tropical gardens and interactive museum visits—but those do not all come with the same ticket rules. Instead of building a schedule around attractive photos, choose one neighborhood and one family priority. This guide compares an easy outdoor day with paid indoor alternatives when heat or afternoon rain makes parks less appealing.

Family planning note

Check each operator's official website for age suitability, opening hours, safety rules, admissions and reservations. Our descriptions do not imply that every location is free or that we have personally visited it.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

What makes sense for your family?

01 · Playground and waterfront views

South Pointe Park

Outdoor park; check splash-pad status

At the southern tip of Miami Beach, South Pointe Park is known for harbor views, paved paths, a children's play area and water-play features. This is a strong choice for a low-pressure morning. Watch children around water and check whether any splash features are operating rather than promising they always run.

02 · Shaded nature and picnic break

North Beach Oceanside Park

Public park; no attraction ticket

This park gives families shaded paths and a calmer environment for outdoor time. It is a different outing from the crowded South Beach corridor. Bring essentials for the weather and check current conditions with the city or destination operator.

Official visitor information ↗Find on Maps ↗
03 · Hands-on science and aquarium exhibits

Phillip and Patricia Frost Museum of Science

Paid admission; check current tickets

Frost Science is a family-focused indoor museum experience with a planetarium and aquarium exhibits. It is not the same institution as Frost Art Museum at FIU. Check age suitability, timed experiences and admission details before building a visit around it.

Official visitor information ↗Find on Maps ↗
04 · Art-focused indoor alternative

Institute of Contemporary Art, Miami

Free admission; reservation guidance applies

ICA Miami is a free contemporary art museum, but children may experience exhibitions differently depending on their ages. Preview the current exhibitions with your family and check the museum's reservation guidance.

Official visitor information ↗Find on Maps ↗
05 · Interactive children's learning

Miami Children's Museum

Ticketed; confirm age-appropriate exhibits

Miami Children's Museum provides hands-on play and learning environments designed for young visitors. It's a good indoor backup to coastal parks. Verify admission, current operating times and any special programs.

A BETTER FAMILY DAY

Three steps before you leave.

  1. For a low-cost morning, choose one park and plan around shade, hydration and age-appropriate play.
  2. For rainy-day indoor time, compare Frost Science, ICA Miami and Miami Children's Museum, which have different ticketing rules.
  3. Do not try to treat South Pointe Park, the Design District and the museums as one compact stroll.
