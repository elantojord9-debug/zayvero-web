Before you visit

Does the $0 filter guarantee a free day?

No. It focuses on no-admission venues or public areas. Transportation, parking, food, extra exhibitions and some events still cost money.

Does this show what's open this weekend?

No. This is an evergreen itinerary planner, not a live events database. Check official opening schedules, holiday closures and booking rules for each location.

Are the pet suggestions dog-friendly?

Not necessarily. The pets filter shows outdoor possibilities and deliberately does not claim a verified pet policy. Always check the operator's rules and local restrictions, especially around wildlife, beaches and special events.

Can I share my plan or save it?

Yes. The share link records your selected city and settings, not personal data. Saved plans remain in your current browser only, with no login or synchronization.
