A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Atlanta
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Trails and Midtown parks

Atlanta has an unusually visible mix of green space and walking infrastructure. Piedmont Park is a flagship public park in Midtown and an easy choice for an outdoor afternoon.

The Atlanta BeltLine Eastside Trail connects several neighborhoods, while Centennial Olympic Park offers a downtown public space with a different atmosphere. Each works better when combined with other stops in the same area.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about Atlanta

What is a good free outdoor activity in Atlanta?+

Piedmont Park and stretches of the Atlanta BeltLine offer public outdoor spaces. Specific events and nearby businesses may charge.

Is the BeltLine one single short trail?+

No. It is a larger network of trails and corridors; choose the segment that fits your route and time.
