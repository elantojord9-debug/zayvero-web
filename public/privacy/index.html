PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

Effective November 8, 2026. This notice explains how Troviora handles data while you explore U.S. city guides.

Information you choose to send

We do not require an account to read guides or use the free planner. When you send a business inquiry or correction through your email app, we receive the email address, venue details and message you provide and use them to reply. The business form prepares a draft in your email app; it does not automatically submit your information to our server.

Saved places, weekend plans and preferences

Saved places, locally saved weekend itineraries and your analytics choice are stored in the browser's local storage. There is no user account or cross-device synchronization. A shared weekend link contains your selected city, planned start date, interests, pace and admission budget preference; it does not contain your name or email. You can remove saved weekend plans in the planner or clear browser storage.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand general site visits, popular content, and interactions. Our measurement ID is G-G1YM981MHV. Analytics does not load until you choose Allow analytics in our notice. If you refuse, Google Analytics is not loaded by Troviora. You may change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices control in the footer.

Google may process device and usage information under its own privacy policy. Our choice banner keeps analytics disabled by default. We do not currently place third-party display advertising cookies.

Hosting and external websites

Our hosting and security providers may process IP addresses, requests and device information to deliver and protect our pages. Linked Google Maps and venue websites are independent services with their own policies and may process data after you follow a link.

Business inquiry submissions

Our online business inquiry link opens a Google Form operated by Google. When you choose to submit it, the information you provide is processed by Google Forms and is accessible to the Troviora account managing the form. We use it only to evaluate and respond to your inquiry. The separate email-draft form does not send data automatically.

Optional weekend newsletter signups

If you choose to subscribe to future weekend updates, a linked Google Form asks for your email address and express consent. The information is processed by Google Forms and can be accessed by the Troviora account managing that form, solely for sending the future emails you request. We are not currently sending an automated newsletter or selling the list. You can later unsubscribe from our emails or request removal by contacting us.

Contact and your requests

For privacy questions or requests about an email you sent us, contact zayverosolutions@gmail.com. We may update this policy if advertising, additional analytics tools or server-side forms are introduced.
