QUICK PLANNING NOTES

Pick a place to start

International Rose Test Garden

A large rose garden in Washington Park gives visitors free outdoor paths, planting displays and views of the city. Parking can cost extra and the City of Portland explicitly requires dogs to stay on leash. Free garden entry. Free public garden admission. Parking can cost extra and access may change for events or maintenance.

See address and official source ↗

Oregon Museum of Science and Industry (OMSI)

OMSI's interactive science spaces are designed for hands-on discovery across several age groups. General entry is paid, and selected exhibitions, presentations or experiences can require additional tickets. Paid museum admission — verify rates. Check the museum's current admission calendar, closures and optional ticket upgrades on its official website.

See address and official source ↗

Powell's City of Books

The famous independent bookstore provides a city-block collection of books to browse at your own pace. Entry does not require a ticket, but shopping and optional purchases are separate costs. Free to browse; purchases cost extra. Browsing has no admission fee. Check current shop hours and any special-event rules on the retailer's official site.
