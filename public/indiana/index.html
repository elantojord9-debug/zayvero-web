QUICK PLANNING NOTES

Pick a place to start

White River State Park

The canal and surrounding downtown green spaces offer an easy walking outing without buying a museum ticket. Families can explore the paths, while neighboring attractions and boat rentals are separate paid choices. Free public park entry. Free park entry, but parking, rentals and attractions may charge. Park rules permit leashed dogs on leashes no longer than six feet.

See address and official source ↗

The Children's Museum of Indianapolis

Hands-on exhibits connect science, history and creativity for families and younger visitors. Prices vary depending on your visit date, so select tickets on the official site before planning a full museum day. Paid admission — prices vary by date. Advance reservations are recommended; verify date-specific rates and closures. Most visits require paid admission.

See address and official source ↗

Indianapolis Central Library

The Central Library brings a historic limestone structure together with a modern glass-and-steel addition. It offers a quiet indoor setting for books and architecture without a general admission ticket. Free public library entry. Entry to the public library is free. Check the official branch page for opening hours, events and individual room restrictions.
