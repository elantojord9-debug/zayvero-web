your way.

✳ Especially good for: Public parks, Tennessee history and landmark architecture

Nashville is often associated with paid live music, but a relaxed city outing can also focus on park scenery and regional history. Centennial Park, Tennessee State Museum and Bicentennial Capitol Mall offer contrasting outdoor and indoor experiences without regular general admission charges.

The two downtown-adjacent history destinations are closer to one another than Centennial Park, which lies to the west near Vanderbilt. A smart plan is to decide which neighborhood fits your day rather than making all three mandatory. Special events, parking and the Parthenon interior have different admission rules.

Centennial Park

Centennial Park provides outdoor paths, green space and views of Nashville's full-scale Parthenon replica. Walking through the public park is free, but going inside the Parthenon museum is a separate ticketed activity.

Address: 2500 West End Avenue, Nashville, TN 37203

Admission: Free general admission

Park grounds are free. The Parthenon interior and selected activities may be ticketed.

Nashville Parks lists a designated dog park within Centennial Park. Follow leash rules outside the designated off-leash area. Official park rules ↗

Tennessee State Museum

The Tennessee State Museum offers exhibitions about the state's history and culture in a purpose-built public facility. General admission is free, and its location beside Bicentennial Capitol Mall makes it a sensible indoor anchor for a history-focused outing.

Address: 1000 Rosa L Parks Boulevard, Nashville, TN 37208

Admission: Free general admission

General admission is free. Confirm museum opening days, holiday closures and special programs.

Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park

Bicentennial Capitol Mall is a public state park that uses monuments, a large map and landscaped paths to tell stories about Tennessee. It offers a natural continuation of a museum visit, though weather, organized events and nearby parking can affect your experience.

Address: 600 James Robertson Parkway, Nashville, TN 37243

Admission: Free general admission

Public state-park access is free. Check official park alerts, programming and any separately charged services.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about Nashville

Are Nashville's Centennial Park grounds free?+

Yes, the outdoor public park is free to explore. The Parthenon museum interior is a separate paid attraction.

Does Tennessee State Museum charge admission?+

No. Its official visitor page states that general admission is free, subject to opening hours and closures.
