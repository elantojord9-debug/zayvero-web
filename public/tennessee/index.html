Why start with Nashville?

The two downtown-adjacent history destinations are closer to one another than Centennial Park, which lies to the west near Vanderbilt. A smart plan is to decide which neighborhood fits your day rather than making all three mandatory. Special events, parking and the Parthenon interior have different admission rules.

A BETTER WAY TO PLAN

Quick planning notes

Centennial Park

Centennial Park provides outdoor paths, green space and views of Nashville's full-scale Parthenon replica. Walking through the public park is free, but going inside the Parthenon museum is a separate ticketed activity. Free general admission. Park grounds are free. The Parthenon interior and selected activities may be ticketed.

See address and source in Nashville ↗

Tennessee State Museum

The Tennessee State Museum offers exhibitions about the state's history and culture in a purpose-built public facility. General admission is free, and its location beside Bicentennial Capitol Mall makes it a sensible indoor anchor for a history-focused outing. Free general admission. General admission is free. Confirm museum opening days, holiday closures and special programs.

See address and source in Nashville ↗

Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park

Bicentennial Capitol Mall is a public state park that uses monuments, a large map and landscaped paths to tell stories about Tennessee. It offers a natural continuation of a museum visit, though weather, organized events and nearby parking can affect your experience. Free general admission. Public state-park access is free. Check official park alerts, programming and any separately charged services.
