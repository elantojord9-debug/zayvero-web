A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Washington, D.C.
your way.

✳ Especially good for: National monuments, free museums and culture

Washington, D.C. has major cultural institutions where the regular price of admission is zero. A memorial, a natural history museum and an art museum each deliver a different experience, so choosing among them is more useful than trying to visit every building around the Mall.

The Lincoln Memorial stands at one end of the National Mall, while the Smithsonian museum and National Gallery are located farther east. Walking distances can be substantial, particularly in summer heat. Verify museum security rules, closures and special events before choosing your route.

01
History & landmarks

Lincoln Memorial

The Lincoln Memorial offers a striking setting for reflecting on U.S. history and the landscape of the National Mall. There is no entrance fee, but access to some facilities can vary, especially during evening hours or public events.

Address: 2 Lincoln Memorial Circle NW, Washington, DC 20002

Admission: Free general admission

No general entry fee. Check National Park Service alerts for temporary closures and facility hours.

Arts & culture
Family-friendly

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History

Explore displays that introduce natural history through collections, scientific research and exhibitions. General admission is free without a pass, making this a practical indoor option, though all visitors must comply with museum screening and access rules.

Address: 10th Street & Constitution Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20560

Admission: Free general admission

General admission is free with no pass needed. Check opening hours, security rules and seasonal closures.

Official visitor information ↗See on Google Maps ↗
03
Arts & culture

National Gallery of Art

The National Gallery connects visitors with art across centuries in its buildings and sculpture spaces. General admission and regular exhibitions are free, while some events may require advance registration despite having no ticket price.

Address: 6th Street & Constitution Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20565

Admission: Free general admission

General admission is always free. Some programs require free registration; verify current entrances.

Questions about Washington, D.C.

Are these Washington, D.C. attractions really free?+

Yes. The three official operators identify free general admission, with separate rules for access and events.
