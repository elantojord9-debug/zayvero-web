Why start with Washington, D.C.?

The Lincoln Memorial stands at one end of the National Mall, while the Smithsonian museum and National Gallery are located farther east. Walking distances can be substantial, particularly in summer heat. Verify museum security rules, closures and special events before choosing your route.

Three different places, not one giant itinerary

Lincoln Memorial, Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History and National Gallery of Art cover different interests. The city page includes full street addresses, an official operator link and a Google Maps search for each destination. Compare them before committing to a route; we do not calculate live travel times.

Free does not always mean zero cost

Public access and free general admission do not cover optional tours, parking, food or paid attractions. When entry is ticketed, our page says so and sends you to the venue's official ticketing information rather than guessing a current price.

A BETTER WAY TO PLAN

Quick planning notes

Lincoln Memorial

The Lincoln Memorial offers a striking setting for reflecting on U.S. history and the landscape of the National Mall. There is no entrance fee, but access to some facilities can vary, especially during evening hours or public events. Free general admission. No general entry fee. Check National Park Service alerts for temporary closures and facility hours.

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History

Explore displays that introduce natural history through collections, scientific research and exhibitions. General admission is free without a pass, making this a practical indoor option, though all visitors must comply with museum screening and access rules. Free general admission. General admission is free with no pass needed. Check opening hours, security rules and seasonal closures.

See address and source in Washington, D.C. ↗

National Gallery of Art

The National Gallery connects visitors with art across centuries in its buildings and sculpture spaces. General admission and regular exhibitions are free, while some events may require advance registration despite having no ticket price. Free general admission. General admission is always free. Some programs require free registration; verify current entrances.
