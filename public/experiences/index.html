COMPARE BEFORE PAYING

Three questions to ask.

What is included?

Tickets, transport, food and extras may be priced separately. Always check the exact product and total before purchasing.

Can you cancel?

Cancellation, refunds and rescheduling depend on the operator and the particular experience, not on a general city list.

Could you do it free?

Sometimes a self-guided park or neighborhood visit offers what you're looking for. Compare our free activities guides first.
