A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Los Angeles
your way.

✳ Especially good for: City views, art and the Pacific

Los Angeles rewards choosing a zone. Griffith Observatory sits above Hollywood and provides city views and science exhibits, while Santa Monica Pier is a very different coastal experience.

The Getty Center is another memorable destination for art and architecture. These highlights are across a large metropolitan area: choosing one or two will usually make a more comfortable day than trying to combine all three.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about Los Angeles

Can you visit Griffith Observatory for free?+

The observatory building and grounds have free admission, while some planetarium programs require paid tickets. Confirm current details with the observatory.

Are the Getty Center and Santa Monica Pier close together?+

They are separate Westside attractions; allow travel time and consider choosing one as your main stop.
