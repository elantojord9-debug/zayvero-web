A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover San Diego
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Coast and cultural parks

San Diego blends coastal scenery with a strong cultural-park tradition. Balboa Park contains museums, public grounds and gardens, so it can easily be the focus of a whole outing.

La Jolla Cove is best approached as a coastal viewpoint and beach area, while Old Town San Diego State Historic Park presents a historic district. Water and wildlife conditions at the coast change; local guidance should always take priority.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about San Diego

Is Balboa Park free?+

You can generally explore outdoor public areas without a museum ticket, but many museums and attractions charge admission.

Can I always swim at La Jolla Cove?+

No. Ocean conditions, posted rules and beach advisories vary; check the current lifeguard guidance before swimming.
