A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover San Francisco
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Bay views and creative neighborhoods

San Francisco is especially good for discovering neighborhoods on foot, though its steep streets and microclimates can catch visitors off guard. Golden Gate Park offers major green space across the western city.

Crissy Field is a waterfront alternative with bridge views, while the Ferry Building Marketplace combines local vendors with the Embarcadero. These places suit different itineraries, and all deserve weather-aware planning.
