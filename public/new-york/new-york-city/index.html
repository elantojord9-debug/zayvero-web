A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover New York City
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Classic urban walks

New York City's great parks are part of its everyday culture. Central Park is a destination in its own right, offering extensive paths and landmarks between neighborhoods.

Bryant Park gives you a much smaller Midtown stop, while the High Line is an elevated public park on the west side. Think of these as three distinct walkable experiences rather than trying to see every part of Manhattan in one day.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about New York City

Are Central Park and the High Line free to enter?+

These are public parks with free general access, though particular programs or nearby attractions may charge.

How long does it take to explore Central Park?+

It depends entirely on the section. Choose a few landmarks and a manageable route instead of planning to cover the entire park.
