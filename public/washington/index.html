Why start with Seattle?

Kerry Park sits on Queen Anne Hill, separate from downtown's Pike Place Market and Central Library. A map and transit check are useful before arranging them in sequence. Market shopping is optional, and public library entry should not be confused with paying to visit a private attraction.

Three different places, not one giant itinerary

Kerry Park, Seattle Central Library and Pike Place Market cover different interests. The city page includes full street addresses, an official operator link and a Google Maps search for each destination. Compare them before committing to a route; we do not calculate live travel times.

Free does not always mean zero cost

Public access and free general admission do not cover optional tours, parking, food or paid attractions. When entry is ticketed, our page says so and sends you to the venue's official ticketing information rather than guessing a current price.

Quick planning notes

Kerry Park

This small Queen Anne viewpoint is known for a classic view of Seattle's skyline and the Space Needle. Plan it as a scenic stop rather than a full-day park, and be mindful of slopes and weather when walking to the overlook. Free general admission. Public park entry is free. Street parking, transportation and special arrangements are separate.

See address and source in Seattle ↗

Seattle Central Library

The public library's downtown building combines reading spaces and contemporary architecture, making it a rewarding stop even for someone who is not borrowing books. Public access is free, but tours, meeting areas and holiday hours should be checked with the library. Free general admission. Public library access is free; parking is paid. Verify current hours and areas open to visitors.

See address and source in Seattle ↗

Pike Place Market

Explore the historic public market district for vendors, stalls and a lively waterfront-adjacent atmosphere. Browsing the public market does not require an admission ticket, but meals, purchases, parking and some experiences are paid. Free general admission. Browsing the public market is free. Vendors, guided experiences, food and parking can cost; merchant hours vary.
