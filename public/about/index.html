ABOUT US

Discover more, right where you are.

Troviora is an independent city-discovery project for residents and visitors across the United States.

Why we're building this

Finding a great local place shouldn't require hours of searching. We organize a growing collection of parks, cultural attractions and public spaces into straightforward city guides with practical context and useful links.

How our directory works

The search lets you select all 50 U.S. states. A city receives a published guide only after its content has been prepared. Our coverage is intentionally limited; we do not claim a live nationwide event database.

Independent recommendations

We link to official operators where possible and don't claim personal visits or firsthand testing that did not occur. Sponsored content will be labeled. Please send corrections to zayverosolutions@gmail.com.
