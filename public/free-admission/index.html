How to spend less without relying on coupons

Choose the neighborhood first

Two places near one another often save more in transport than an advertised ticket discount. Check route distances in a real map before leaving.

Read entry rules carefully

Some free museums still require reservations. Separate general admission from paid exhibitions, parking, food and special programs.

Verify discounts with the seller

Student, resident and age discounts are not universal. Confirm eligibility, total prices and cancellation rules directly with each official provider.

For optional guided experiences, compare the provider's current ticket terms on our tour research page. The public Viator links there are not yet affiliated or commission-paying.
