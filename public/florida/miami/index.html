A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Miami
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Waterfront, street art and gardens

Miami has several completely different personalities. Downtown's Bayfront Park is a waterfront green space, while Wynwood is a neighborhood known for contemporary murals and creative businesses.

Vizcaya Museum & Gardens takes visitors toward an entirely different setting: historic architecture and formal gardens. Mixing all three in one day is possible only with careful travel planning, so it is often better to choose one or two.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about Miami

What can I see in Miami without booking a tour?+

Bayfront Park is an outdoor starting point. Wynwood is a neighborhood to explore, though entry into its named art attraction may require tickets.

Is Vizcaya free?+

Vizcaya Museum & Gardens is generally a ticketed attraction. Always check its official visitor information before going.
