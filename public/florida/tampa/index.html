A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Tampa
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Waterfront walks and family outings

Tampa's Riverwalk makes the waterfront easier to explore on foot, connecting urban parks and public attractions along the Hillsborough River. Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park is a useful central meeting point.

The Florida Aquarium is an indoor, ticketed contrast to the free-to-walk outdoor promenade. A simple approach is to enjoy the riverfront first and add one paid attraction only if it fits your budget.

Questions about Tampa

Is Tampa Riverwalk free to walk?+

The public promenade is an outdoor pedestrian route. Nearby businesses, attractions and parking can charge separately.

What is a good family indoor activity in Tampa?+

The Florida Aquarium is one established indoor option; admission is ticketed.
