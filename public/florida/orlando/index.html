A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Orlando
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Beyond the theme parks

Orlando is not only about theme parks. Lake Eola Park introduces the downtown lakefront, while Leu Gardens offers a botanical visit a short distance from the central district.

The Orlando Science Center gives families a different indoor option. For a lower-cost day, start with the open public spaces, then decide whether a ticketed garden or science museum is worth adding.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about Orlando

Are there things to do in Orlando besides theme parks?+

Yes. Lake Eola Park, Leu Gardens and the Orlando Science Center offer very different experiences beyond the major resort areas.

Is Orlando Science Center free?+

It is a ticketed museum for general admission. Check its official website for offers, special events and current pricing.
