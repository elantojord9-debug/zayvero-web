TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Advertising & affiliate disclosure.

Troviora is an independent city-discovery website, not a ticketing agent or an operator of the venues we list.

Independent editorial listings

We select and describe places using available official sources. A business does not have to pay to be considered for inclusion. Paid sponsorships, if offered in the future, will not guarantee a positive editorial review.

Affiliate links

We may earn a commission from certain third-party links once affiliate partnerships have been approved and enabled. We will identify those relationships close to the relevant recommendations. At present, our experiences page links to standard non-affiliate Viator destination pages, which do not generate tracked commissions for us.

Display advertising

We are preparing the site to apply for display advertising programs, but we do not currently serve AdSense ads. If advertising is activated, it will be labeled or otherwise recognizable as advertising and we will update the privacy controls as required.

Sponsored business features

We may eventually sell clearly labeled sponsored features to local businesses. Inquiry forms are for early contact only; no subscription or checkout is active. Paid arrangements will state the price, deliverables and term before an agreement.
