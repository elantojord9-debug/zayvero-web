Why start with Las Vegas?

Bellagio's conservatory closes briefly between seasonal exhibits, so a visitor should never assume the current installation is available. Fremont Street has public entertainment alongside paid businesses. Springs Preserve is a separate ticketed destination rather than a free extension of the Strip.

Three different places, not one giant itinerary

Bellagio Conservatory & Botanical Gardens, Fremont Street Experience and Springs Preserve cover different interests. The city page includes full street addresses, an official operator link and a Google Maps search for each destination. Compare them before committing to a route; we do not calculate live travel times.

Free does not always mean zero cost

Public access and free general admission do not cover optional tours, parking, food or paid attractions. When entry is ticketed, our page says so and sends you to the venue's official ticketing information rather than guessing a current price.

Quick planning notes

Bellagio Conservatory & Botanical Gardens

The Bellagio conservatory presents large seasonal arrangements of plants and themed displays near the hotel lobby. Public viewing is complimentary, but the space may close for installation changes between seasons, making the current calendar essential. Free general admission. Free general viewing when a display is open. Closed during announced changeover dates; check the current schedule.

See address and source in Las Vegas ↗

Fremont Street Experience

The downtown pedestrian entertainment district offers canopy light displays and public live performances. Walking through the area and many regular shows are free, while zipline rides, casino activities, food and special-ticket events cost extra. Free general admission. General pedestrian access and regular public performances are free; parking and paid activities are separate.

See address and source in Las Vegas ↗

Springs Preserve

Springs Preserve combines desert-focused exhibitions and outdoor areas in a destination away from the busiest resort corridors. General admission is ticketed, and outdoor activities can be curtailed in extreme heat, so check rates and visitor alerts before choosing a date. Paid entry. Paid general admission for most visitors; resident discounts and child rules vary. Confirm current price and availability.
