✳ Especially good for: Free entertainment, seasonal gardens and desert learning

Las Vegas has more variety than ticketed shows and casino entertainment. A hotel garden display, a downtown pedestrian entertainment area and a museum-and-desert-preserve complex make three very different outings, with different costs and distances.

Bellagio's conservatory closes briefly between seasonal exhibits, so a visitor should never assume the current installation is available. Fremont Street has public entertainment alongside paid businesses. Springs Preserve is a separate ticketed destination rather than a free extension of the Strip.

Bellagio Conservatory & Botanical Gardens

The Bellagio conservatory presents large seasonal arrangements of plants and themed displays near the hotel lobby. Public viewing is complimentary, but the space may close for installation changes between seasons, making the current calendar essential.

Address: 3600 S Las Vegas Boulevard, Las Vegas, NV 89109

Admission: Free general admission

Free general viewing when a display is open. Closed during announced changeover dates; check the current schedule.

02
Arts & culture

Fremont Street Experience

The downtown pedestrian entertainment district offers canopy light displays and public live performances. Walking through the area and many regular shows are free, while zipline rides, casino activities, food and special-ticket events cost extra.

Address: Fremont Street, Las Vegas, NV 89101

Admission: Free general admission

General pedestrian access and regular public performances are free; parking and paid activities are separate.

Official visitor information ↗See on Google Maps ↗
03
Parks & outdoors
Family-friendly

Springs Preserve

Springs Preserve combines desert-focused exhibitions and outdoor areas in a destination away from the busiest resort corridors. General admission is ticketed, and outdoor activities can be curtailed in extreme heat, so check rates and visitor alerts before choosing a date.

Address: 333 S Valley View Boulevard, Las Vegas, NV 89107

Admission: Paid admission — check official prices

Paid general admission for most visitors; resident discounts and child rules vary. Confirm current price and availability.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about Las Vegas

Is the Bellagio Conservatory free?+

Bellagio describes complimentary public viewing, but seasonal changeover periods can temporarily close the exhibit.

Is Springs Preserve free?+

No. It sells general admission tickets, with certain discounts and free admission for some very young children; verify prices on the official page.
