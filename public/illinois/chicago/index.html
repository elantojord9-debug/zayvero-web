A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Chicago
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Public art and lakefront walks

Chicago's downtown lakefront makes public art and architecture easy to encounter. Millennium Park is a recognizable introduction near the Loop, and the Riverwalk is a separate downtown pedestrian experience.

Lincoln Park is farther north and suits a different outing with lakefront green space. Wind and seasonal conditions matter, so selecting a compact route is often more enjoyable than trying to see every landmark at once.

Questions about Chicago

Can you visit Millennium Park for free?+

The public park is generally accessible without an entry ticket, but individual events or special experiences can have separate rules.

What is a good walk in downtown Chicago?+

Millennium Park and a section of the Chicago Riverwalk are two convenient downtown starting points.
