A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Austin
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Parks and Texas history

Austin is especially rewarding outdoors, with many beloved public places clustered near the Colorado River. Zilker Metropolitan Park provides a broad introduction, with gardens and recreation nearby.

The State Capitol offers a contrasting look at Texas history and architecture. Lady Bird Lake is a good choice for an active walk, with access points and trails that deserve a little planning.
