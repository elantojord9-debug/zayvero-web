A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Dallas
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Arts district and urban parks

Dallas' city center combines public art, walkable districts and a distinctive park built over a freeway. Klyde Warren Park is a useful starting point between the Arts District and Uptown.

The Dallas Museum of Art brings an indoor option nearby, while White Rock Lake is better for a longer outdoor escape away from the central core. Decide first whether your day is about arts or fresh air.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about Dallas

What can I do in downtown Dallas?+

Klyde Warren Park and the surrounding Arts District make a practical starting point; the Dallas Museum of Art is nearby.

Is White Rock Lake close to downtown Dallas?+

White Rock Lake is a separate destination east of central Dallas, so allow additional travel time.
