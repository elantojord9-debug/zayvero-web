A BETTER WAY TO EXPLORE

Discover Houston
your way.

✳ Especially good for: Downtown green spaces and outdoors

Houston is spread out, so a good day starts with choosing one area rather than racing from one side of the city to another. Downtown's Discovery Green is an easy introduction to the city's public spaces; the Museum District and Hermann Park are a different kind of outing.

Buffalo Bayou Park offers a longer, more active waterfront experience. These three picks cover distinct moods: a downtown stroll, a garden-and-museum day, and a scenic trail. This is a planning guide, not a live event schedule.
