OUR STANDARDS

How we choose what to feature.

Useful, accurate guides come before rankings and advertising.

Selection

We feature recognizable public places, museums and destinations that offer distinctive ways to explore a city. Descriptions are researched summaries, not verified first-person reviews.

Sources and updates

We prioritize venue operators and official resources for descriptions. Current opening hours, access, prices, closures and events should always be checked with the operator.

Corrections

If information is outdated, send the city, place and corrected source to zayverosolutions@gmail.com.

Advertising independence

Editorial inclusion cannot be bought. Any future paid placements or affiliate links will be marked clearly.
