## Publishing guidelines
- Format: 9:16, clean captions, high-contrast text, 15–30 seconds; make the first two seconds useful.
- Source visuals: actual licensed local destination photos, original graphics, or footage you have rights to use. Never imply AI-made imagery is a real venue photograph.
- Give each post ONE deep link to the relevant article; use a short URL or link in profile where direct links are unavailable.
- Publish only from accounts actually authorized for Troviora. Do not create fake reviews or fabricated visitor testimonials.
- Pinterest: use vertical 2:3 graphics with a readable title and relevant source link. Avoid a repeated generic city skyline as the sole graphic for every post.
- Each caption should say admission/availability are subject to operator confirmation when appropriate.
- Track clicks in GA4 (consent-first) and use Search Console to choose future content; do not promise rankings.
